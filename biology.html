    WHAT THIS VERSION DEMONSTRATES

    Wings produce forces. Forces move the body.

    Two wing surfaces generate lift, drag and body moments. Gravity, inertia, airflow and attitude feedback determine the trajectory. Switching off the wings removes their support; the fly falls. The drone learns with its own independent model.

    WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH

    A fruit fly is not a mosquito.

    This reduced-order model uses published reference dimensions and frequency, with explicitly assumed wing shape, aerodynamic coefficients and controller gains. It has not been fitted to held-out biological trajectories. It is not the complete MuJoCo flybody model, a brain emulation, or evidence of household safety.
